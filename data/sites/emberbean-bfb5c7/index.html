Asked often

Good questions

When do you roast?

Every Thursday morning. Bags ship or sit on the shelf within 48 hours of the roast.

Do you grind for home brew?

Yes — bring your method (V60, AeroPress, espresso) and we grind to match.

Where are you?

Behind the arches of the old roastery lane in Marrow Bay — knock during opening hours.

How do I reach you fastest?

Email hello@emberbean.site or call +1 (321) 590-9226. We answer within one working day.

Do you do gift options?

Yes — ask at the counter and we will wrap it properly.
