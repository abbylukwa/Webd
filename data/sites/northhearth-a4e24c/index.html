Good questions

When should I come for pastries?

Trays land at 8am sharp; cardamom knots are gone by 9:30 on weekends.

Do you take loaf pre-orders?

Yes — order by 4pm for next-morning pickup, held until noon under the counter.

Where are you?

Behind the arches of the corner bakery in Kirkwall — knock during opening hours.

How do I reach you fastest?

Email hello@northhearth.site or call +1 (785) 605-2059. We answer within one working day.

Do you do gift options?

Yes — ask at the counter and we will wrap it properly.
